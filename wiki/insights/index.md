* [Scope words and multipliers inflate between a vendor's text and its retellings](launch-claims-inflate-between-source-and-retelling.md) - In three checked cases, a retelling claimed more than the primary source: Duet's scope, xerj's 40x, and a 1/83 cost claim. Check scope words and multipliers against the first-hand text before quoting.
* [A proactive agent is defined by where its approval boundary sits and what happens when it fails](proactive-agents-are-defined-by-their-approval-boundary.md) - Vendors draw the line at irreversible actions and money. In this tracker the gate fails in three places: the user approves too broadly, the agent never asks, or the monitor is weaker than claimed.
* [Verification, not generation, is now the bottleneck, and vendors answer by moving the review](verification-is-the-bottleneck-not-generation.md) - Agents write faster than people can review. Evidence from Anthropic, a Harvard paper and PostgreSQL shows review time rising, and three different answers: verify intent, prove properties, or hand unreviewed reports to the receiver.
